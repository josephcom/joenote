# Take note or not?
**Yes**! Why? Because **we *see* and *learn* and *remember* pictures, not individual dots and individual lines which connect them**. The same dot can contribute to different pictures. We can detect and create different pictures out of the same dots. Each dot can become part of multiple pictures. 

A text is noisy; meaning it's filled with irrelevant dots and messy lines connecting them. Main pictures are burried in them and very difficult to 1) detect 2) remember them. You have to take them out.
