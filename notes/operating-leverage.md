# Operating Leverage
## Calculation
**Example**  
Revenue: $1,000
Variable cost: $600
Left over: $400 ← this is contribution margin
Fixed cost: $300
EBIT (profit before interest and tax): $100
True leverage = 400 ÷ 100 = 4×
