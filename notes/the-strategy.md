# THE strategy

**Step 1 (Set the limit).** Leave 85% of your money in VGS and do not touch it. Set aside 5% of your total money for this adventure. If it goes well over time, only then you can ramp up slowly. 

**Step 2 (Screen to find candidates).** Search the market for companies that have paid unusually high dividends over the last five years, not just one lucky year.

**Step 3 (Why so good?!).** For each one, find out why the dividend is so high. The honest answer is usually that something is wrong with the business.

**Step 4 (What should change?).** Work out what would have to change for the share price to recover, and write those changes down clearly before you get attached to the story.

**Step 5 (Set your exits).** Also write down:

1. the highest price you would still pay
2. what would prove you wrong
3. a date when you give up and delete the idea

**Step 6 (Add to the radar).** Add it to your list and check on a set schedule.

**Step 7 (Never more than 1%).** Keep each company to 1% of your total money; never more!

**Step 8 (Beware of "correlation").** Make sure those companies do not all depend on the same thing.
