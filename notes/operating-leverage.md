# Operating Leverage
The easiest way to calculate it is by comparing each year's revenue and profit with that of its previous year. The following tables are real numbers from ASX:GEM:

| Year | Revenue   | Operating EBIT | Δ Rev | Δ EBIT | Leverage  |
| ---- | --------- | -------------- | ----- | ------ | --------- |
| FY22 | $905.5m   | $80.3m         | +4.5% | +0.2%  | **0.05×** |
| FY23 | $987.0m   | $100.6m        | +9.0% | +25.3% | **2.8×**  |
| FY24 | $1,021.8m | $115.0m        | +3.5% | +14.3% | **4.1×**  |
| FY25 | $948.2m   | $93.3m         | −7.2% | −18.7% | **2.6×**  |

From the above table you can calculate both **fixed costs** and **break-even revenue**:

> Fixed cost = EBIT × (leverage − 1)
