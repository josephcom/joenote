# GEM

They rent rooms + educators, then accept kids to educate them. educator per room is fixed by law, whether 1 kid shows up or 20. So, they have a *fixed* cost. Revenue, however, is so wobbly! They charge per day per kid by the way. Their margin is also so thin. In FY25, from $948m revenue, only $93m survived as operating profit — a skinny ~10% margin.
