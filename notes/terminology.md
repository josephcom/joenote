# Terminology
**market cap** — Share price × number of shares. What the whole company costs to buy today. GEM: 770.65m shares × 13.0c ≈ $100m.

**net debt** — What it owes the banks, minus the cash it holds. The debt you'd inherit as an owner. GEM: $124m — more than the company itself is worth.

**operating EBIT** — Earnings Before Interest and Tax: profit from just running the business, before the bank and the taxman, and with one-off items stripped out. GEM: $14.7m.

**statutory line** — The official bottom line under accounting rules, one-off nasties included. The number the law requires, as opposed to the "operating" number management prefers to talk about. GEM: −$38.8m.

**real wages** — Pay rises minus inflation. Negative means pay went up but prices went up more, so households can buy less than a year ago.
