# Margin Calls

Groves and other directors in ABC Learning had borrowed money using their own ABC shares as security. **Rule of a margin loan**: if the share price falls, the bank demands instant repayment — a margin call — or it force-sells your shares. Remember this.

The fall, 2008. February: half-year profit drops 42%. The price crashes over 40% in a day. That triggers the margin calls. The founders' shares get dumped onto a falling market, crushing the price further. A death spiral in one week. August: the new auditors refuse to sign the accounts — past "profits" have to be re-stated as losses. The shares freeze at around 54 cents. They never trade again.
