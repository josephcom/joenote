# Take note or not?

**Yes!** 

## Patterns
Because **we *see* and *learn* and *remember* pictures, not individual dots and individual lines which connect them**. The same dot can contribute to different pictures. We can detect and create different pictures out of the same dots. Each dot can become part of multiple pictures. 

A text is noisy; meaning it's filled with irrelevant dots and messy lines connecting them. Main pictures are burried in them and very difficult to 1) detect 2) remember them. You have to take them out. 

Each question we ask is a picture. You have to extract the right dots and lines to answer it. 

## Unknown unknowns 
You can't give a book to AI and ask questions without missing ***invaluable*** unknown unknowns. Unknown unknowns are *gaps* in your knowledge. Filling them *permanently* improves your judgements.

## Bottom-line 
Here's how you should take note:
> 1. Put each point in the text under one of more questions.  
> 2. Set aside a separate section for unknown unknowns
