# Accounting

Imagine a lemonade stand.

Money in the jar. Every day, coins come in from customers and go out for lemons. What's left in the jar is *cash*.

Profit is a story, not a jar. Accountants don't just count coins. They add promises ("Bob will pay me Tuesday" counts as income *today*) and subtract pretend costs ("my juicer got older, that's -$50" — but no coin left the jar).

So the two numbers differ. Profit = income earned minus costs incurred, *on paper*. Operating cash flow = coins that actually entered and left the jar from selling lemonade.

Profit (EPS) tells you the story; cash tells you the truth. Check both.
