# Fundamental Analysis: Steps
1. Understand the business model, break-even revenue (are they making or losing money? any *going-concern* warning?), operating leverage etc.
2. Competitors, market share, compare others market cap, share price and understand why.
3. What things would affect the demand side and how do we *predict* (leading indicators)?
4. What things would affect the supply side and how do we *predict* (leading indicators)?
